The method

Every change sets off a second,
slower process: the transition.

Change happens on a date: the new job, the signed papers, the reorg announcement. The transition it sets off happens inside you, over months, and that slower process is the part almost nobody plans for. The KIT Model© is a step-by-step way to manage it.

  1. K

    Knowledge

    Understand your landscape before you try to cross it. What has actually shifted, what you've lost, and what forces are in play. Knowledge removes the fog that makes change so disorienting.

  2. I

    Integration

    Change hits you emotionally, cognitively, relationally, and practically, all at once. Integration weaves those together instead of treating them separately. Insight becomes action.

  3. T

    Transformation

    Transformation means using the transition as raw material for growth. Every transition is an incubator for it, and this is where that growth takes root.

The questions people actually ask

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Twenty minutes, no cost, and no obligation to book anything afterwards. Every engagement starts this way because neither of us can tell whether it's a fit until we've talked.

Is this therapy, or is it coaching?

It can be either. Dr. Musamali is a Licensed Professional Counselor-Supervisor, so clinical counseling is available for individuals, couples, and families. Consulting and coaching for professionals and organizations are separate services with a different shape. Part of what the first call decides is which of them fits you.

Do you meet virtually?

Yes. Sessions are available in person in the Dallas-Fort Worth area and virtually anywhere, with scheduling that includes evenings and weekends. Clinical counseling is limited to clients located in Texas; consulting, coaching, training, and speaking are not.

I'm not in crisis. Is it too early to reach out?

Earlier is better. Transition work is most effective before a change has finished doing its damage. If you're anticipating something rather than recovering from it, that's an excellent time to start.

What does it cost after the consultation?

It depends entirely on what you need. A handful of sessions, an organizational engagement, and a one-day workshop are priced very differently. You'll get clear numbers on the call, before you're asked to decide anything.
